ABOUT

When I started this little project, I wasn’t just trying to build something — I was trying to connect the dots between the things I was learning. At the time, I was practicing DOM manipulation: figuring out how to grab elements, et values, and update them through input sliders. It felt like a puzzle, and every small step (like getting the slider to show the right value) gave me a sense of progress. But I didn’t want it to stop at just the code. Around the same time, I was learning about typography, visual hierarchy, and UX/UI principles — proximity, alignment, padding, margin. So, I decided to fold all of that into this experiment. By adjusting sliders, I could literally see how spacing and alignment change the look and feel of a design. In the end, it became a fun way to bring coding and design together in one place. A simple idea, but one that reminded me why I’m excited about becoming both a web designer and developer.

And honestly, although my design skills arent perfect, working on this made me realize something important:

I think I really love design and want to explore it deeply.
